# Lee's Method

**Lee’s method** experiment demonstrates how to determine the coefficient of thermal conductivity of a poor conductor by establishing steady heat flow through the material. In this method, heat is supplied to a metallic disc–specimen–metallic disc arrangement until a steady temperature difference is maintained. After removing the heat source, the rate of cooling of the lower disc is measured. By equating the heat conducted through the specimen at steady state to the heat lost by the disc during cooling, the thermal conductivity of the material is calculated.
