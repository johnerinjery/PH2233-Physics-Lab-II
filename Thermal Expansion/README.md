# Coefficient Of Thermal Expansion

The aim of the thermal expansion experiment is to determine the coefficient of linear expansion of a given metal rod by measuring its change in length with temperature. As the rod is heated, the expansion is recorded using a spherometer arrangement while the corresponding temperature is noted. By plotting length change (or instrument reading) against temperature and calculating the slope, the coefficient of linear expansion is obtained using
$$\alpha = \frac{1}{L_{0}}\frac{dL}{dT}$$
$L_{0}$ being the initial length of the rod.
